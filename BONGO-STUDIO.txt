BONGO CAT'S TINY STUDIO 0.4.0-rc1
Custom instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1. Built 23 October 2026.

A LITTLE BAND, ALL YOURS
Turn PRESETS for your sound and ALGORITHM for Lofi / Funk / Arcade.
PLAY starts the band. SELECT or HOME cycles twelve pages. The four knobs control
what that page shows. MASTER always controls the output volume.

PAGE        KNOB 1          KNOB 2          KNOB 3          KNOB 4
Studio      Tempo 40-220    Swing           Band energy     Room reverb
Harmony     Key            Major/minor     Progression     Key assist
Looper      1/2/4 bars      Replace/overdub Quantize/feel   Metronome
Mixer       Your cat       Keys cat        Bass cat        Drum cat
Dress up    Coat           Hat             Room colour     Calm/lively
Help        Metronome      -               -               -
Sounds      Part           Engine          Preset          Part level
Tone        Sound control 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 (labels follow the engine)
Colour      Sound control 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 (labels follow the engine)
Envelope    Attack         Decay           Sustain         Release
Effects     Drive          Chorus          Delay           Reverb
Live        Your cat       Keys cat        Bass cat        Drum cat
            Each knob: PLAY / MUTE / SOLO. ENV taps tempo; LFO cancels solo.

EDIT EVERY CAT
Go to SOUNDS with SELECT / HOME. K1 chooses Your Cat, Keys, Bass or Drums;
K2 chooses among 13 engines, K3 browses its factory presets, K4 sets its level.
Each part can use any engine. Turning a sound/preset control preserves that
part's sequence, mixer level, pan and mute, and leaves all other parts alone.
On SOUNDS, TONE, COLOUR, ENVELOPE and EFFECTS, PRESETS browses this cat's
presets and ALGORITHM changes this cat's engine. On the original six pages
they still choose your eight lead instruments and the band style.
TONE and COLOUR expose all eight engine controls, including waveform, filter,
kit/sample and FM6 patch controls where that engine provides them. Labels and
units follow the chosen engine and mode. ENVELOPE changes ADSR; EFFECTS edits
only this part's drive, chorus, delay and reverb. One-shot drum sounds also
have their own shaping controls in TONE/COLOUR.
The panel keyboard auditions the selected cat on these editor pages. External
MIDI keeps its existing channel routing. REC returns to the lead for recording;
you cannot switch audition parts during a take. Your sound edits remain
independent of the sequence and survive SAVE, autosave and power-on.
LFO on a sound-editor page swaps that cat between its current sound and the
previous edit (Pawdo A/B), including its own FM6 patch. A continuous knob sweep
is one edit; a pause of 750 ms or a different control begins another. Each cat
has its own undo. Notes, level, pan and mute stay put. Undo is not saved.
The browser also has direct page and part buttons. Editor page/part selection
is temporary; the actual sounds and all their parameters are saved.

FIRST MUSICAL JAM
1. HOME once: Harmony. Pick C minor, COZY and IN KEY with the four knobs.
2. HOME again: Looper. Pick 2 or 4 bars, REPLACE and QUANTIZED. Leave click OFF.
3. SELECT back to Studio. Choose soft keys, marimba or space bell with PRESETS.
4. Press REC while stopped. Watch the four lead-in dots for one bar. When
   I'M LISTENING appears, play. The take ends automatically and starts looping.
5. Choose OVERDUB on Looper and press REC to add notes at the next phrase start.
   Existing notes play while you overdub. LFO restores the previous full take.
6. Mix the band on Mixer, then SAVE to stop and remember the whole jam.

RECORDING
REC while stopped gives a one-bar visual lead-in. While playing, it waits for
the next loop boundary. The recording lasts exactly the chosen phrase length.
The display shows the current bar, a 16-step tape and four beat dots while
armed. REC again ends a take early. REPLACE clears the previous phrase when
recording begins. OVERDUB adds notes using Felucca's four-notes-per-step limit;
held notes can replace following steps with ties. AS PLAYED keeps recorded
timing; QUANTIZED aligns playback to the swung step grid.
Change phrase length while stopped. Growing duplicates the current phrase;
shrinking shortens playback. LFO undoes either change, including all 64 steps.
EDIT clears the entire lead phrase; LFO undoes. Undo is one level, not saved.

HARMONY THAT MAKES MUSICAL SENSE
The band stays in your chosen key instead of following your last melody note.
HOLD repeats the tonic. COZY: I-vi-IV-V. DREAM: I-IV-vi-V. JAZZ: ii-V-I-vi.
Each chord lasts a bar. In minor, the same scale degrees use natural minor;
JAZZ's ii is diminished and its v is minor. Bass supplies the root, with two
upper notes choosing nearby inversions so the accompaniment moves smoothly.
Energy adds rhythmic details, velocity accents and occasional fills.
Bass notes now sustain up to a beat, with the same pitches and start times.
New band sounds use a warm harmonic bass, with dry sustain and a gentle
160 ms dip when the kick hits. This leaves the kick transient clear.
Existing jams keep their saved sound settings when loaded.
Key assist snaps the physical/panel keyboard into the chosen major/minor key.
External MIDI pitches stay as sent, so your DAW/controller retains control.
It does not rewrite notes already recorded. Drums ignore scale assistance.
Old jams keep their original key, one-bar length, HOLD harmony and free keys;
enable COZY and IN KEY on Harmony when you want the new arrangement features.

LIVE PERFORMANCE
LIVE gives each cat its own PLAY / MUTE / SOLO knob. Levels stay where you
set them. Mutes are saved; solo is temporary and overrides the original mutes
without erasing them. LFO on LIVE clears solo and restores that original mix.
ENV on LIVE is tap tempo: tap quarter notes, 40-220 BPM. It averages up to four
intervals. A pause resets it; external MIDI clock stays in charge when selected.
Mutes and solo fade smoothly, preserving effect tails. PRESETS and ALGORITHM
are inactive on LIVE so a performance gesture cannot replace your sounds.

BUTTONS AND PAGE SHORTCUTS
PLAY       Start / stop the band and loop.
REC        Arm one take, or finish the current take early.
ARP        Band on/off at the next bar. SEQ mutes/unmutes the lead loop.
FX held    Maximum Cat: drum fills, flying hearts, big paws and a light chase.
EDIT       Clear lead phrase. LFO restores the last take/clear/resize outside
           the editor and Live; on editor pages LFO swaps sound A/B.
SCL        Major/minor. ENV cycles hats (tap tempo on Live). GLO cycles rooms.
OCT -/+    Live keyboard octave. SAVE stops and remembers the studio.
PRESETS    Bongos, soft keys, meow, chiptune, marimba, drum kit, bass cat, bell.
ALGORITHM  Lofi, Funk, Arcade outside the sound editor. Changes its engine inside.
PRESETS and ALGORITHM act on the selected cat on all five sound editor pages.
Choosing a band style intentionally loads its three accompaniment sounds.
Leave ALGORITHM alone on the original pages to keep your custom band sounds.

A CAT WITH PERSONALITY
Cream, tuxedo and calico coats; bare ears, headphones, beret, shades, crown
or flower. Midnight, mint, strawberry and coffee rooms. Blinks, gentle beat
bobs, recovering paws, pitch glances and floating hearts follow your notes.
The three bandmates react to their own parts and dim when muted.
Hold FX from any page for a full-stage party; release returns to your page.
Calm mode keeps this celebration still.
Finish a phrase for a little celebration. Three secret musical phrases wake
the Jazz Police, open the Cat Cafe or send a Purrbit into orbit. Each is visual
only: it never changes your sound, tempo or recording. See EASTER-EGGS.txt
for the optional spoilers. They work transposed and time out between phrases.
Calm mode disables the extra bobbing, blinks and hearts; note feedback remains.
The meow is a playable formant synth. All art is procedural and original.

METRONOME
Click and Felucca's audio count-in are OFF on every boot. Looper KNOB 4 changes
OFF / RECORD / ON for the session. Help KNOB 1 does the same. The browser has
an explicit selector. This does not mute the band. The visual lead-in remains.
The physical FM-1's USB audio excludes the click; its speaker/headphone DAC
includes it. USB recordings cannot establish the speaker's metronome state.

SAVING / MIGRATION
SAVE waits for silence and uses Felucca's redundant CRC-checked autosave.
Idle autosave remains enabled. The phrase, instrument, style, mixer, harmony,
loop settings, tempo, swing, reverb, coat, hat and room restore on next boot.
Version 0.1 jams migrate automatically. Restoring a Bongo archive through
Felucca's backup protocol also restores its studio controls and saves the jam. Compact settings use a BC2 + BASE32
project-name tag inside the existing format, with no new flash layout.
The four explicit project slots are not used. Page, solo, undo and animation state
are temporary. Reinstalling 0.1 after saving in 0.2 needs the old backup to
recover the old metadata; 0.1 cannot interpret the new BC2 tag.

BROWSER PREVIEW / AUDIO EXPORT
Open http://127.0.0.1:8766/ and click Switch on. The same C audio, sequencer,
controls and renderer run as WebAssembly. Page buttons and live knob captions
help you learn the physical controls. Click/drag knobs or use the keyboard.
Record WAV captures up to 90 seconds of the browser instrument's stereo audio
at 44.1 kHz / 16-bit. Stop & save WAV downloads the performance. It records
no microphone. This is separate from the physical FM-1 and its saved jam.
The demo plays a short melody in the current key using your current sound; it
keeps your sound and hat. It is unavailable while a take is armed/recording.
You can capture the demo with Record WAV.
Run locally: python3 -m http.server 8766 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory build/emu

INSTALLATION / RECOVERY
Package: bongo-cat-studio-0.4.0-rc1.fwsc in the release bundle.
Identity FM-1_980; editor INFO BONGO CAT 0.4.0-rc1. USB name remains Felucca.
The existing Felucca updater, bootloader and OCT- + OCT+ recovery are retained.
Use the normal Felucca installer, without forcing a package. See INSTALL.txt
in the release bundle for its tested route, backup commands and recovery notes.
The release's VERIFICATION.json records its evidence and limits; keep your
own pre-update backup and the previous working firmware for recovery.
This focused instrument replaces Felucca's panel editor. External editor
parameter edits are outside its workflow. USB MIDI note input remains.
