BONGO CAT'S TINY STUDIO 0.4.0-rc1
Release candidate / 23 October 2026

A four-part instrument with a tiny band on its screen. Play a lead, record a
phrase, shape every sound, and perform the arrangement with mute and solo.
The cat reacts to your playing. The music stays under your control.

NEW IN THIS CANDIDATE
- Live page: individual Play / Mute / Solo, smooth fades and preserved levels.
  Solo is temporary; clearing it brings back the original mute states.
- Tap tempo on Live (ENV), averaging up to four quarter-note intervals.
  External MIDI clock remains authoritative.
- Pawdo sound A/B (LFO on the five editor pages): a separate previous edit
  per cat, including custom FM6 voices. Notes and the mixer are preserved.
- Jazz Police, Cat Cafe and Purrbit musical Easter eggs, in any transposition.
  Original procedural artwork; visual only, with CALM support.
- Save/error/undo messages now appear on the editor pages too.
- Replace/overdub mode is locked during an armed or active take.
- Browser inputs preserve their order through startup and fast page changes.
  Responsive layouts keep the selected page captions when the window changes.
- Browser demo keeps your sound/hat and refuses to start during recording.
  Browser storage failures are visible, and an unsupported sample rate fails
  clearly instead of playing at the wrong pitch.

KEPT FROM 0.3
13 engines on every part, factory presets, eight engine controls, ADSR and
per-part effects. 1/2/4-bar recording, overdub and phrase undo. Key assistance,
four progressions, diatonic backing and voice leading. Sustained warm bass
with kick ducking. Maximum Cat, dress-up, quiet startup and stereo WAV export.

UPGRADING
The existing BC2 jam format and flash layout are unchanged. Back up the live
jam with the current firmware before updating. The normal Felucca updater is
used, without force. Identity is FM-1_980; USB still appears as Felucca.
A/B, solo and Easter eggs are temporary; sound edits and mute settings save.
Downgrading to 0.3 understands the same project format; keep the backup.

VERIFICATION AND RELEASE STATUS
This is a release candidate. VERIFICATION.json in the release bundle records
what was actually tested, including package simulations, sanitizer tests,
browser tests and physical FM-1 evidence when available. Tests do not replace
a person exercising every physical control or a long hardware soak.
Do not describe this candidate as tested on multiple FM-1 hardware revisions.

KNOWN BOUNDARIES
- Shared polyphony and CPU budget apply to the four engines; demanding patches
  can reduce available voices. One short hardware jam is not every patch mix.
- Keyboard recording is for Your Cat; backing is generated, not a four-track
  arbitrary pattern editor. EDIT clears the lead; LFO outside Live/the sound
  editor restores the previous take, clear or resize.
- ALGORITHM on the original six pages deliberately loads a whole band style.
  Use the sound editor to change one cat. Live disables those sound selectors.
- Play/Mute/Solo keeps effect tails. Mutes save, solo does not.
- The four explicit Felucca project slots have no panel workflow in this UI.
- The browser preview has its own jam; it does not edit the attached FM-1.
- USB recording excludes the metronome. DAC click behavior is tested separately.
