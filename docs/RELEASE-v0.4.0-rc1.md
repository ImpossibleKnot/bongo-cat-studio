# 0.4.0-rc1 — The cats have unionised 🐾

The first public Tiny Studio candidate from **[Impossible Knot](https://github.com/ImpossibleKnot)**: a four-part cat band for the M-VAVE FM-1, built on Felucca 1.6.1.

**Start with `bongo-cat-studio-0.4.0-rc1.zip`.** It includes the firmware, exact corresponding source (with engines), installation/backup tools, compiled browser preview, licences and verification reports. Read `INSTALL.txt` inside before flashing. `SHA256SUMS` below checks the three release downloads; the ZIP also has its own internal checksums.

## The staff have new powers

- Edit every part with 13 engines, eight engine controls, ADSR and effects.
- Record 1/2/4-bar lead phrases with visual count-in, overdub and undo; the three backing cats follow your key, progression and energy.
- Use the Live page for smooth mute/solo and external-clock-aware tap tempo.
- Swap each cat's sound A/B with Pawdo, including custom FM6 patches.
- Hold FX for Maximum Cat: drum fills, giant paws, flying hearts and a light chase.
- Discover Jazz Police, Cat Café and Purrbit through secret musical phrases. Their paperwork is purely visual.
- Play the actual firmware in the included browser preview and export stereo WAVs.

## Install and preserve your jam

The tested physical route is **Tiny Studio 0.3.0 → 0.4.0-rc1**, on one FM-1. Make a verified backup first and retain your previous working firmware. For other starting firmware, follow its documented migration/recovery route. A Felucca archive is for returning to Felucca, not a Bongo jam import.

Package identity: `FM-1_980`. USB MIDI/audio name: **Felucca**. Normal Felucca updater/loader and recovery remain. The metronome starts off.

## Evidence, without cat maths

Six sanitizer suites, all 4 parts × 13 engine editor checks, phrase/undo/save/cold-boot tests, 2,000 live gestures, real browser WASM/AudioWorklet tests and exact-package update simulations passed.
