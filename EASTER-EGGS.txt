TINY STUDIO / OPTIONAL SPOILERS

These are quiet visual rewards. They add no notes, change no sound and never
rewrite a recording. Play one note at a time, with less than 1.8 seconds between
notes. Use a melodic lead sound. FREE key assist makes the examples literal;
external MIDI also works. All three phrases can be transposed.

JAZZ POLICE
D E F G E C D. A little gold jazz badge appears: PAWS UP.

CAT CAFE
C A F E. The cat opens its tiny cafe, with a heart mug and rising steam.

PURRBIT
A C E A E. A small ringed planet and stars visit the studio.

Each lasts five seconds. Stay on Studio to see the scene. CALM freezes the
extra motion. Notes and their usual performance feedback still work.

Hold FX on any page for Maximum Cat: giant paws, little bandmates, hearts,
confetti and a light chase. It is momentary; release returns to your page.
